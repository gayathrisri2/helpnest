Common Questions

Helpful answers to get you started.

What is HelpNest?

HelpNest is a customer support ticket system that helps users submit issues and track their progress in one place.

How do I create a support ticket?

Open the Create Ticket page, enter the issue title, category, priority, description and contact email, then submit the form.

How can I track my ticket?

Visit the My Tickets page to view your tickets. Select a ticket to see its full details and current status.

What do the ticket statuses mean?

Open means the ticket is awaiting action. In Progress means work is underway. Resolved means the issue has been addressed. Closed means the ticket is no longer active.

What does ticket priority mean?

Priority indicates how urgently an issue needs attention. HelpNest provides Low, Medium, High and Urgent options.

Can I search for a ticket?

Yes. Open My Tickets and use the search field to look for a ticket by its title or ticket ID.

Where can I find ticket updates?

Open the Notifications page to view updates generated by the HelpNest application.

What should I do if I forget my password?

For this demo version, contact the project administrator if you cannot log in. A secure password-reset feature can be added to a production version.

How can I contact the support team?

Visit the Contact page and submit your question through the contact form.

Are my tickets stored permanently?

In the browser demo, ticket data can be stored in local storage on the same browser. A real deployment requires a backend and database for shared, persistent storage.
